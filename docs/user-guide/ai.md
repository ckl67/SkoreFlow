# And What About AI?

Artificial Intelligence cannot be ignored today anymore, and played a supporting role throughout the development of this project whilst maintaining a curious approach.
AI was used to occasionally provide code suggestions, propose alternative approaches, and help identify oversights or omissions.

However, AI-generated contributions always required a thorough review, validation, and adaptation process. Maintaining full control over the architecture, design choices, and implementation remained essential to ensure that the final result reflected a coherent, maintainable, and human-driven approach.

The only area where I placed a somewhat higher degree of trust in AI assistance was the user interface, particularly when working with `Tailwind CSS`. While there is still significant room for improvement in terms of visual design and user experience, I must acknowledge that AI substantially accelerated the design process and helped me achieve a satisfactory result much faster than I could have on my own.

Ultimately, AI was a valuable tool, but never a substitute for understanding, craftsmanship, and ownership of the codebase.
